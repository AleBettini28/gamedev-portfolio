Apocalypse Island is a First Person Open-world Survival game, you play as a scientist on a mission to find a missing person on a toxic island full of zombies. The game was released on Steam the 8th June 2025 after 4 months of Early Access and roughly 7 month of development time.

This game is the biggest project I have ever done and I had so much fun working on it. Being unable to make 3D models I used Synty Studios Assets to create the world and the weapons. I learned a lot about 3D Unity features, Navmeshes, animations, 3D movement, enemy AI, world building and so much more.
