The Secret of Dr. Maximilian P. Rillo is a 2.5 D narrative game where you play as Dr. Rillo, the son of Dr. Maximilian P. Rillo, embarking on a journey through time to find the missing pages of your father's diary and uncover the greatest scientific secret about time travel.

The game was made in the span of 10 days for the April Fools' Day Jam 3, in which with this game I ranked 8th out of 55 partecipants. I was really proud of this game, expecially because it was my first time working in a 2.5D view and writing dialogues or a complete storyline, even if short.
