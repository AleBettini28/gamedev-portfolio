IT Expert

My name is Alessandro Bettini, I am 19 years old and I live in Northern Italy. I’ve been studying Computer Science for the past five years at the Saraceno Romegialli Institute, and I graduated this year.

When I first chose this school, I didn’t have a strong passion for computer science. But as I began to understand its potential, I fell in love with it—so much so that I now want to turn it into my career.

Over the course of my studies, I’ve learned to program in various languages. In my second and third years, I worked mainly in C, developing small mathematical applications. Although this wasn’t my favorite part of the curriculum, I still found it interesting. In my fourth year, we moved on to Java, which I enjoyed right away. While many classmates and even some teachers saw Java GUI development as one of the worst parts of programming, I’ve always liked it. In fact, graphical interfaces were what made that year the most engaging and enjoyable for me.

In the summer of 2023, driven by a mix of curiosity and boredom, I started exploring game development using Unity and C#. I created several small games and eventually published my first full 2D title, Death Box, on itch.io in the summer of 2024.

Over the following year, I continued working on more games and gradually moved into the 3D world. In June 2025, after about seven months of development, I released Apocalypse Island, a first-person survival game, on Steam.

Currently, I’m working on several smaller projects, including a fast-paced FPS for the 2025 S&box Game Jam.
